Style Guide

How to layer necklaces without cluttering your look

Layering works best when you combine different lengths, keep one focal piece, and repeat a similar metal tone for balance.

How to store jewelry properly

Keep pieces dry, separate them in soft pouches, and avoid mixing delicate chains with heavier items.

Choosing a gift for her

If you are unsure, choose timeless studs, a minimal bracelet, or a pendant necklace in a classic finish.

When to wear statement pieces

Statement earrings or bold rings work best when the rest of the outfit stays clean and simple.
